WakeStorm — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026
Effective from the initial public release of WakeStorm.
Developer: AK Studios
Privacy contact: akstudios.support@gmail.com

1. Summary

  • WakeStorm does not require a user account and does not operate a developer-controlled backend server.
  • Alarm settings, history, statistics, morning checklist, world-clock list and sound-file references are stored locally, subject to the Android backup settings described below.
  • WakeStorm is free and supported by Google AdMob banner and native advertisements. Google and its partners may process device data for advertising as described in section 5. Advertising requests are subject to applicable consent requirements and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Where available, advertising privacy choices can be accessed through the app's privacy settings.
  • Advertisements are not displayed during alarm ringing, wake-up challenges, on the lock screen or in notifications.

4. What WakeStorm does not do

WakeStorm does not require sign-in or request access to contacts, device location, microphone or messages. It does not access files except where needed for files you explicitly select, such as alarm sounds. The developer does not sell personal data or operate a separate analytics or crash-reporting service. Third-party SDK data practices are described below.

5. Third-party services

Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK

Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as IP address, approximate IP-derived location, advertising or device identifiers, device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostic information. Purposes include ad delivery, measurement, frequency management, security and fraud prevention, and personalisation where permitted. Data handling and sharing depend on the SDK configuration, applicable law and privacy choices.

See Google's Privacy Policy, AdMob privacy guidance and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure. You can manage advertising ID and personalisation settings on supported Android devices and use in-app privacy choices where available.

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

UMP manages advertising privacy messages and stores consent choices. Advertising requests are subject to the consent state and applicable requirements. Where consent is required, users can manage or withdraw it through the available privacy options.

Google ML Kit (bundled barcode scanner)

Barcode decoding takes place on the device. Google ML Kit may collect limited technical, usage or performance information as described in its Android data disclosure guidance. WakeStorm does not upload barcode camera images.

Google Play services and Android components

Google SDKs and Android system components may process information under Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

6. Legal bases, retention and your rights

Where applicable, advertising processing that requires consent relies on that consent. Other processing may rely on applicable legal bases depending on the purpose and service. Local processing supports the features you request. Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or obtain a copy of your personal data.

For local app data, use the controls in section 2. For data processed by Google, use Google's privacy and account tools, or contact us for assistance. Google handles its own retention and deletion practices under its privacy policy.

If you contact AK Studios by email, we receive the information you choose to provide and use it to respond to your inquiry. We retain support correspondence only as needed to handle the inquiry and meet applicable legal or security obligations. You may contact us to request deletion of correspondence, subject to those obligations.

7. Children

WakeStorm is not specifically directed to children under 13 or the applicable minimum age in their jurisdiction. The developer does not knowingly collect personal data from children. Please contact us if you believe a child has provided personal data to us.

8. Security

WakeStorm uses Android's app-private storage for local app data. Google SDK network communications use security measures described in the relevant SDK disclosures. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as app features, SDKs or requirements change. The date shown above will be updated when the policy is revised. Where required, we will provide notice of material changes.

10. Contact
