1. Summary

  • Wake Storm does not require a user account and does not operate a developer-controlled backend server.
  • Alarm settings, history, statistics, morning checklist, world-clock list, sound-file references, sleep records and selected personal media are stored locally, subject to the Android backup settings described below.
  • Wake Storm is free and supported by Google AdMob banner, native and interstitial (full-screen) advertisements. Google and its partners may process device data for advertising as described in section 5. Advertising requests are subject to applicable consent requirements and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Where available, advertising privacy choices can be accessed through the app's privacy settings.
  • Advertisements are not displayed during alarm ringing, wake-up challenges, on the lock screen or in notifications.

Optional sleep and snoring features

When you explicitly start a sleep session and enable listening for possible snoring, Wake Storm uses the microphone to analyse audio on your device, including while the screen is off. Short clips of possible snoring events may be saved in app-private storage. Recording is optional, requires microphone permission, and displays an ongoing notification with a stop control. You can stop recording at any time. Recording stops when an alarm rings and does not automatically restart after a device reboot. Audio is not transmitted by Wake Storm to AK Studios or used for advertising. Android system backups are described below.

You can review and delete saved clips and configure a retention limit. Automatic expiry checks may not run immediately when a clip reaches that limit; use the deletion controls to remove clips immediately from local app storage. Sleep and snoring information is an estimate for general awareness, not a medical diagnosis or a substitute for professional medical advice. Wake Storm is not a medical device.

Personal media and challenges

Photos and videos you choose through Android's file or photo selection interface may be copied to app-private storage for use as backgrounds. A reference photo registered for Photo Match is saved locally. Camera images captured for matching are processed in memory and discarded after comparison. Barcode images are decoded on the device. Motion sensor readings used for walking, shake and squat challenges are processed on the device; live sensor readings are not stored or transmitted by Wake Storm. Challenge outcomes may be stored in alarm history. Wake Storm does not upload your selected media, reference images, camera captures or sensor readings to AK Studios or use them to personalise ads. See Android backup below.

Local data is retained as needed to provide the features you use until you remove it or clear app storage. You can delete individual items using the applicable feature controls, including alarm history, sleep history, snoring clips and personal media, or clear app storage or uninstall the app using Android settings. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local app data, but does not necessarily remove existing system backups or the original media in your gallery.

Android backup: Depending on Android, your device settings and the app's backup configuration, Android's backup and restore service may include eligible app data, including sleep records, snoring clips, selected backgrounds and reference photos. This can involve a cloud backup managed by your system backup provider or transfer to another device. AK Studios does not operate this service or receive these backups. Clearing app storage or uninstalling does not necessarily delete an existing cloud backup. You can manage device backups in Android or your backup provider's account settings.

4. What Wake Storm does not do

Wake Storm does not require sign-in or request access to contacts, device location permissions or messages. It does not access files except where needed for files you explicitly select, such as alarm sounds, photos and videos. The developer does not sell personal data or operate a separate analytics or crash-reporting service. Third-party SDK data practices are described below.

5. Third-party services

Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK

Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as IP address, approximate IP-derived location, advertising or device identifiers, device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostic information. Purposes include ad delivery, measurement, frequency management, security and fraud prevention, and personalisation where permitted. The app displays banner, native and interstitial ads; it does not display App Open ads. Data handling and sharing depend on the SDK configuration, applicable law and privacy choices.

See Google's Privacy Policy, AdMob privacy guidance and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure. You can manage advertising ID and personalisation settings on supported Android devices and use in-app privacy choices where available.

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

UMP manages advertising privacy messages and stores consent choices. Advertising requests are subject to the consent state and applicable requirements. Where consent is required, users can manage or withdraw it through the available privacy options.

Google ML Kit (bundled barcode scanner)

Barcode decoding takes place on the device. Google ML Kit may collect limited technical, usage or performance information as described in its Android data disclosure guidance. Wake Storm does not upload barcode camera images.

Google Play services and Android components

Google SDKs and Android system components may process information under Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

6. Legal bases, retention and your rights

Where applicable, advertising processing that requires consent relies on that consent. Other processing may rely on applicable legal bases depending on the purpose and service. Local processing supports the features you request. Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or obtain a copy of your personal data.

For local app data, use the controls in section 2. For data processed by Google, use Google's privacy and account tools, or contact us for assistance. Google handles its own retention and deletion practices under its privacy policy.

If you contact AK Studios by email, we receive the information you choose to provide and use it to respond to your inquiry. We retain support correspondence only as needed to handle the inquiry and meet applicable legal or security obligations. You may contact us to request deletion of correspondence, subject to those obligations.

7. Children

Wake Storm is not specifically directed to children under 13 or the applicable minimum age in their jurisdiction. The developer does not knowingly collect personal data from children. Please contact us if you believe a child has provided personal data to us.

8. Security

Wake Storm uses Android's app-private storage for local app data. Google SDK network communications use security measures described in the relevant SDK disclosures. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.
